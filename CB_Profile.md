## 4. SAP Community Involvement & Recognition

### **SAP Mentor**

* **Handle:** `@CBasis`

> **What is an SAP Mentor?**
>
> SAP Mentors are a select group of top influencers, community leaders, independent experts, and practitioners recognized directly by SAP. Founded in 2011 to bridge community experts with SAP leadership, they act as a vital feedback loop between the SAP ecosystem (customers, partners, developers) and SAP product/engineering teams, providing candid product feedback, driving innovation, and fostering community learning.
